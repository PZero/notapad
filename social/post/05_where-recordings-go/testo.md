## Didascalia (da incollare)

You record your whole day. Where does it go?

Here is how we are designing notªpad: audio stays on your devices, speech-to-text runs on your phone, and an open MCP server hands your notes to the AI you choose. Once your AI has them, they are deleted from the server. Prefer nothing in the middle? Run the server yourself: it is open source too.

What would you want an AI to do with your day? A to-do list, a diary, meeting minutes? Tell us 👇
