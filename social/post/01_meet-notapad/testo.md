## Didascalia (da incollare)

Meet notªpad: a tiny resin monolith that remembers your day.

It records with two microphones, syncs to your phone over Bluetooth and lets the AI you choose transcribe and organise your notes through an open MCP server. No subscription, no locked-in cloud.

Hardware, firmware and app are fully open source. We're two makers building it in public, and the first prototype boards are about to be ordered.

Follow along for every step: board, firmware, resin, first recording. 🎙️

🔗 Project page and all files: https://pzero.github.io/notapad/
