## Didascalia (da incollare)

The brain of notªpad: 16.5 × 38 mm, four copper layers, every part on one side.

Top: charging contacts and a microphone that listens for speech. Middle: three RGB lights, 256 MB of storage, front microphone, touch and motion sensors. Bottom: the Bluetooth module, with a clear zone for its antenna.

We did not draw it by hand: Python scripts generate the schematic, route the board and write the factory files, and 71 automated checks run before every order.
