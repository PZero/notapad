## Didascalia (da incollare)

Your best ideas are said out loud. Then they are gone.

AI recorders exist, but they keep your days in their cloud, pick the AI for you and charge every month. We think a device that listens to your life should belong to you: your data, your AI, open hardware and software, no subscription.

If you might use one someday, your opinion matters more than anything else. We are two makers, not a company, and the design is still open: we would rather build what you need than guess. So tell us honestly: would you wear it all day? What would worry you? What would make you trust it? 👇

Want to help more? Share it with someone who loses ideas, chip in on Ko-fi, or, if you are a company, help us build the first 3–5 prototype boards: notapad.team@gmail.com
