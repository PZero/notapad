**Description:**
notªpad is an open-source wearable AI voice recorder: a small resin monolith you wear around your neck. It records your day, syncs over Bluetooth and lets the AI you choose transcribe and organise your notes through an open MCP server. No subscription, no locked-in cloud.

We are two makers building it in public: board design, firmware, resin casting and the first working prototypes. Hardware CERN-OHL-S-2.0, software MIT.
